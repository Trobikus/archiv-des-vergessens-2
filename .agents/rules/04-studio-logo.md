# Studio logo

When a studio logo / brand mark is needed (nav, favicon, Open Graph, schema.org, about/studio, email headers):

- **Full logo:** `site/assets/studio-mark.png` (book + GRIMOIRE / INTERACTIVE)

Do **not** reintroduce the old triangle SVG sigil as the studio logo. Game-specific art (`archiv-cover.png`, `keyart.png`, `hero.png`) stays for game/atmosphere contexts only.
